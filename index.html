About

I'm a backend engineer with nine years of experience across fintech and infrastructure. These days I care most about the unglamorous parts of distributed systems: idempotency, backpressure, and the exact semantics of a timeout.

Before moving to industry I did graduate work on consensus protocols at NTU, where I learned that most "impossible" results are really just precise definitions. I still try to bring that attitude to production incidents.

Outside of work I help organize the Taipei Systems Reading Group, and I'm slowly restoring a 1983 Mendini bicycle.
